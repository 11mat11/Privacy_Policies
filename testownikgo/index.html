Policy Summary: TestownikGO operates strictly as an offline educational tool. The Application does not collect, process, transmit, or monetize any personal data. All operations execute entirely within the local runtime environment of your device, and no network connectivity is established.

1. Scope and Entity Identification

This Privacy Policy constitutes a formal legal statement governing the data handling practices of the mobile application TestownikGO (Package ID: com.s11mat11.TestownikGO, hereinafter referred to as the "Application"), created, published, and maintained by 11mat11 (hereinafter referred to as the "Developer", "we", "us", or "our"). This policy outlines our commitment to user confidentiality and regulatory compliance across global distribution channels, including the Google Play Store.

2. Data Collection and Processing Principle

The Application is architected under the principle of strict data minimization and total offline functionality:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect, capture, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII), such as legal names, residential or electronic mail addresses, phone numbers, precise or coarse geolocation data, or hardware serial identifiers.
  • No Network Connectivity: The Application operates entirely offline and does not establish connections to external servers, cloud databases, or remote endpoints. No inbound or outbound network transmission occurs during the usage of the Application.
  • Zero Remote Telemetry & Advertising: The Application does not integrate any third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) for the purpose of tracking, user profiling, advertising, telemetry, or remote crash reporting (such as Google Firebase, Google Analytics, or AdMob).

3. Local Device Storage and File Handling

Any data processed by the Application remains strictly confined to the local storage environment of the user's physical device:

  • User-Selected Files: Question archives (such as .txt and .zip files) are accessed solely upon explicit user action via the native system file selector. These files are parsed in local volatile memory and remain strictly on the host device.
  • Local Session State & Preferences: Quiz progress, repetition counts, and configurable settings are stored exclusively in the host device's sandboxed local application storage. This data is never synchronized with any remote system.

4. Data Retention, Control, and Deletion

Because the Developer maintains no central infrastructure or remote databases, all stored data is entirely under the user's direct custody:

  • Users may permanently purge all stored preferences, test states, and cached files at any time by clearing the Application's storage via the device's operating system settings.
  • Uninstalling the Application completely and permanently removes all associated application data and cached files from the device.

5. Global Privacy Regulations (GDPR & International Standards)

In accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation / GDPR) and comparable international privacy statutes:

  • Because no personal data is collected, processed, or transferred, the Developer does not act as a Data Controller or Data Processor within the meaning of the GDPR.
  • No automated decision-making, behavioral profiling, or cross-platform tracking is performed.

6. Protection of Children’s Privacy

The Application complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and European Union age-appropriate design codes. Because no personal data is gathered from any individual, the Application does not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 within the European Economic Area).

We reserve the right to revise or amend this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological updates or evolving legal standards. Any modifications will be posted directly to this public document with a revised "Effective Date". Continued use of the Application signifies acceptance of any updated terms.
